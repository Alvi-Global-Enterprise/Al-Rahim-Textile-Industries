Privacy Policy

How Al Rahim Textile collects, uses, and protects your information.

01 Privacy Policy

Your privacy at Al Rahim Textile

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Al Rahim Textile Industries (“Al Rahim Textile”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Who we are

Al Rahim Textile is a manufacturer and exporter of towels, bedding, and kitchen linens, established in 1991. Our corporate office is at G-33, Block 6, P.E.C.H.S, Shahra-e-Faisal, Karachi, Pakistan. Our factory is at A-188, Road #5, S.I.T.E., Nooriabad, Pakistan.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Company name, contact person, country, telephone, fax, and email address submitted through our inquiry form
  • The content of your inquiry or other messages sent to info@alrahimtextile.com
  • Email addresses submitted through newsletter or CTA forms on this website

We may also collect limited technical data automatically, such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, to keep the website working and to improve it.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to product and business inquiries
  • Communicate with existing and prospective customers and partners
  • Improve our website, products, and customer service
  • Meet legal, compliance, and record-keeping requirements

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing of information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate this website or process inquiries, and only as needed for those purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Al Rahim Textile.

5. Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

6. Data retention

We keep inquiry and contact information for as long as needed to handle your request, maintain a business relationship, or meet legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

7. Data security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact us
