Towel & Bedding Textile Company

Al Rahim Textile Industries is one of the largest exporters of home textile products from the region. Shaping the plain fabrics into weaved masterpieces in form of terry towels and bed sheets etc., we have matchless expertise in crafting 'made-to-order' products with all kind of fabrics including Dobby, Jacquard and yarn-dyed fabric.
